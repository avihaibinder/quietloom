# Privacy Policy — Quietloom: Living Sleepscapes

**Last updated: [DATE]**

Quietloom ("the app") is published by **[YOUR NAME]**. This policy explains what the app does and does
not do with information. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

The short version: **we do not have a server, we do not have your data, and there is nothing to log
in to.** The one exception is advertising, which is served by Google, and that is described in
detail below.

---

## 1. No account, no sign-in

Quietloom has no user accounts, no registration, no login, no email capture and no social sign-in. You
never tell the app your name, email address, phone number, date of birth or location, because there
is nowhere in the app to enter them.

## 2. What is stored on your device

Everything the app remembers is stored **locally on your phone**, in the app's own private storage,
and never leaves the device. That is:

- your saved mixes and the last mix you were listening to
- your settings — sleep-timer length, whether the timer is on, the end chime, the nursery-safe
  volume cap, your breathing pattern preference, reduced-motion preference
- whether you have already seen the introduction
- whether a "night pass" is currently active, and when it expires
- whether a "Premium forever" purchase has been unlocked
- the date on which an interstitial ad was last shown, so the app can honour its
  one-ad-per-day limit

None of this is transmitted anywhere. We cannot read it. If you uninstall the app, all of it is
deleted with the app. There is no cloud backup of it operated by us, though your device's own
Android backup settings may include app data — that transfer is between you and Google, under
Google's terms, not ours.

## 3. We collect nothing ourselves

We operate no servers, no accounts database, no crash-reporting service and **no analytics SDK of
any kind**. There is no Firebase Analytics, no Google Analytics, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel, no
Facebook SDK and no attribution or install-tracking SDK in this app. We do not know how many times
you opened Quietloom, which sounds you chose, or how long you slept.

## 4. Audio and the microphone

**Quietloom never records audio and never listens to you.** The app has no microphone permission at
all — you can verify this yourself on the app's page in Google Play, under "App permissions".
Because the permission is absent, the app is technically incapable of accessing the microphone.

Every sound Quietloom plays is generated mathematically on your device, in real time. The app
contains no audio recordings, and it does not download, stream or upload any audio.

## 5. Advertising — the one place data is involved

Quietloom is free, and the free version is supported by ads served by **Google AdMob**. To serve those
ads, Google's advertising SDK — not us — may collect and process information including:

- your device's **advertising identifier** (Android Advertising ID)
- device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language, coarse
  location inferred from your IP address, and app version
- ad interaction data such as whether an ad was shown, viewed or clicked

We never see this information in an identifiable form. What we receive from Google is aggregate
earnings reporting — totals and counts, with no individual users in it.

Where ads appear, and where they deliberately do not:

- A **banner** appears only on the mixer screen.
- An **interstitial** may appear at most once per calendar day, when the app is opened.
- A **rewarded video** is only ever shown if you choose to tap the button that offers one, in
  exchange for unlocking the full sound library for the night.
- **No ads are ever shown in bedside mode, in the breathing pacer, or on any sleep surface.** An
  unexpected loud advertisement at midnight is a genuine harm, and the app is built not to do that.

Your choices:

- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation entirely, in
  **Settings → Google → Ads** on your Android device.
- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app will present
  a Google-provided consent form before personalised ads are used, and you can change your choice
  later from the app's settings.
- Purchasing **Premium forever** removes all advertising from the app permanently, which also ends
  the collection described in this section.

Google's own privacy policy governs everything Google collects through the SDK:
**<https://policies.google.com/privacy>**

Google's description of how it uses data from its advertising partners:
**<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>**

## 6. Purchases

If you buy "Premium forever", the transaction is handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. Your
payment details go to Google and never to us. We receive no card numbers, no billing address and no
name. The app stores only a local flag recording that the purchase exists.

## 7. Permissions the app requests, and why

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet, network state | Required to fetch ads and to check purchase status. The sound engine itself works fully offline. |
| Wake lock | Keeps the screen from sleeping in bedside mode. |
| Foreground service / media playback | Lets audio keep playing when you leave the app or lock the screen. |
| Post notifications | Shows the playback notification while audio is running. |
| Advertising ID | Required by Android 13 and later for AdMob to serve ads. |

There is no microphone permission, no camera permission, no contacts permission, no precise location
permission and no storage permission.

## 8. Children

Quietloom is designed for adults and is **not directed at children**. It is not enrolled in Google
Play's Designed for Families programme, and its content rating and target-audience declaration in
Google Play reflect an adult audience.

We are aware that parents use sound apps near sleeping infants, and Quietloom includes a "nursery-safe"
volume cap for exactly that reason, based on World Health Organization night-noise guidance and
American Academy of Pediatrics guidance on sound machines. That feature is for a parent operating
the phone — it does not make Quietloom a children's app, and a child is not the intended user.

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. We collect no personal
information from anyone. Ad content is limited to Google's **"General"** maximum content rating, so
mature and age-restricted advertising categories are excluded.

If you believe a child has used the app and that information about them has somehow been collected,
contact us at the address below and we will act on it promptly.

## 9. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information, because we do not hold any. The only
third party that receives anything is Google, in its roles as advertising provider, app store and
payment processor, as described above.

## 10. Data retention and deletion

Because all app data lives on your device, you delete it by clearing the app's storage
(**Settings → Apps → Quietloom → Storage → Clear data**) or by uninstalling the app. There is no
account to close and no deletion request to submit to us. For data held by Google in connection with
advertising, use the Google controls linked in section 5, or Google's privacy tools at
<https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy>.

## 11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA or
similar laws — to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to processing.
Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to produce or erase. For data
processed by Google as part of advertising, exercise those rights directly with Google using the
links above, or write to us and we will help you route the request.

## 12. Security

The app stores its settings in Android's per-application private storage, which other apps cannot
read. It makes no network requests other than those made by Google's advertising and billing SDKs,
all of which use encrypted connections.

updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
