# Growth Lead

Get Quietloom in front of people, and turn attention into revenue without damaging
the thing that makes people stay. Owns the store presence, the launch, and the
numbers that actually matter.

## Does not own

Any claim about what the sounds do — that belongs to the Research Lead, and store
copy goes past them before it ships. Ad placement rules are product decisions, not
growth levers.

## The pitch

Every sound is synthesised in real time, so it never loops and never repeats — and
every sound cites the study behind it, including the studies that disagree with us.
Zero audio files means zero licensing cost at any scale, and a tiny download.

The nearest competitor on positioning is **Sonora**, which markets "science-backed
soundscapes" built by an acoustic scientist. Their claim is atmospheric; ours is
per-sound, linked, badged by strength, and publishes the counter-evidence. That gap
is the whole differentiation, so it has to be visible in the first screenshot.

## Naming, already settled

"Drift" was rejected: Google Play carries five sleep apps using it, plus more on iOS,
and `com.drift.sleepscapes` implied ownership of a domain belonging to Drift.com, Inc.
Application IDs are permanent after publication.

Quietloom checked clean across both stores and trademark records. **A full USPTO
clearance search is still outstanding** and should happen before any money goes into
branding.

## Money, honestly

Illustrative, not a forecast. Full arithmetic and assumptions are in `BUSINESS.md` §2.

| Cumulative installs | ≈ DAU | Low ARPDAU | Mid | High |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1,000 | ~50 | $4.50/mo | $14/mo | $30/mo |
| 10,000 | ~500 | $45/mo | $143/mo | $300/mo |
| 100,000 | ~5,000 | $450/mo | $1,425/mo | $3,000/mo |

Read the low column as the likely one. At 1,000 installs you may never clear AdMob's
$100 payout threshold — treat that stage as validation, not income. 100,000 installs
without paid acquisition needs a genuine viral moment; do not plan around it.

**Retention is the only real lever.** It sets DAU, and DAU multiplies every other
number in the table. Ad-format tuning moves things by percent; retention moves things
by multiples.

## Things that would make the numbers worse

- Ads on sleep surfaces. Short-term ARPDAU, long-term one-star reviews.
- Dropping the grace rule. It would raise measured ad completion and lower trust.
- Declaring children as a target audience in Play Console. That triggers Families
  policy, which bans standard AdMob serving. The nursery-safe cap is a *feature for
  parents*, not a children's-audience declaration.

## Sequencing

1. **Play Console first.** A new personal developer account needs 12 testers opted in
   continuously for 14 days before production access. Nothing else can compress that.
2. Real AdMob IDs and `app-ads.txt` — the app earns exactly $0 until this is done.
3. Store listing built around the research angle, with the evidence card in shot 2.
4. Launch: Hacker News is the best-shaped opportunity here ("I synthesised every sound
   and cited every study"). Reddit has real self-promotion rules — read each
   subreddit's before posting or you will be banned rather than upvoted.

the differentiation is invisible and the positioning needs to move, not the copy.
